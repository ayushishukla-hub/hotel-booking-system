#Statement of work - Hotel Room Booking Management System

## Problem Statement
Managing hotel room vacancies, tracking active bookings, and handling updates or cancellations manually leads to operational inefficiencies and recor errors.

## Scope of the Project
The scope includes a console-based Python application that tracks three room categories (Regular, Deluxe, Suite), monitors room status (Vacant vs Booked) and carries out CRUDE modification features (bookinf,updating,and cancellations)

## Target Users
*Hotel Front Desk Clerks
*Administrative Staff

## High Level Features
*Room inventory tracking (Vacant/Booked segregation)
*Automated booking allocation
*Reservation updates and cancellation handling
